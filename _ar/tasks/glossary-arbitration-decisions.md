# glossary arbitration decisions

human arbitration log for terminology conflicts in the Patronus AR pass.

Records decisions when a term is source-backed but semantically disputed (e.g. the same czech label
means different things in different process areas, a CZ/RO/MD status alias maps to more than one
canonical state, or a current-state term collides with a target-state (`it-zadani`) term). Until a
conflict here is `approved`, the disputed term stays out of `_ar/repo-map/glossary-master.csv`.

## status values
