---

## Conventions

- **One MSG = one distinct message *type*, not one Notification-Matrix row.** The ~117 matrix rows
  collapse onto role × status × channel variants of a much smaller set of message contracts; the
  status fan-out email/in-zone messages (MSG0005 / MSG0006) are the deliberate catch-alls for every
  status row not broken out into a higher-salience dedicated message.
- **Channels:** email is delivered via **ES0006** (Mautic); *in-zone* notifications are delivered
  through the platform's internal party-zone display (Parent Zone / Patron Zone / Donor Zone) —
  internal, **not** an external system. Slack (**ES0015**) and Telegram (**ES0016**) are **ops
  alerting**, not user-facing transactional messages, and are intentionally **out of scope** for MSG.
- **Source FN** lists the capability that *produces/gates* the message; **FN0019** (dispatch &
  templating) is the shared transport capability behind every emailed message and is listed
  alongside the producing capability rather than repeated in prose.
- **Related EN** cites the subject entity/entities; **EN0022** (EmailArchive) is cited on every
  emailed message as the send-record subject. Entity attributes and business-rule content are
  **cited, not restated** (cross-layer discipline §1–§3).

## Coverage summary

- **30 message types** documented, covering the full user-facing transactional surface across the
  process areas: application intake & two-sided completion (MSG0001–0002, 0007), access/auth
  (MSG0003–0004), the grouped status fan-out (MSG0005 email / MSG0006 in-zone), risk & coordinator
  decisions (MSG0009–0012), application/story cancellation (MSG0016–0018), story lifecycle &
  collection outcome (MSG0013–0016), donations & recurring (MSG0019–0023), vouchers (MSG0024–0025),
  contract & signature (MSG0026–0027), tax confirmation (MSG0028), and post-campaign feedback
  (MSG0029–0030).
- **Grouped catch-alls:** MSG0005 (status email) and MSG0006 (in-zone status) absorb every
  Notification-Matrix status row not broken out for a higher-salience intent — the ~117 rows are
  **not** exploded into ~117 files (see synthesis report §4).
- **Explicitly excluded from MSG:** ops alerting on Slack (ES0015) / Telegram (ES0016) — not
  user-facing — and all delivery/template/config mechanics (owned by FN0019 and cited, never
  restated). See synthesis report §6.
