## OQ-01 — Two divergent application-entry URLs: `/zadost/zadatel` vs `/zadost-formular` — **Conflict**

**Gap ref:** G-16 (audit §b/§c-6). **Verdict: Conflict — requires clarification.**

Two distinct front doors are observed for the same capability (submit an application, **UC0001**):

- **`/zadost-formular`** — a **5-step wizard** (1 Příběh · 2 Dar · 3 O Vás · 4 Patron · 5 Přílohy);
  step 1 is "Váš příběh" (child identity + story + health/nationality/parallel-collection flags).
- **`/zadost/zadatel`** — a **different first step** "Údaje o žadateli": only **e-mail + phone + one
  GDPR consent**, and a **"Zpět na výběr"** back-link implying a **prior selection step** before this
  one (a pre-wizard contact gate, or an alternate/legacy path).

**Why it matters for the rebuild.** These may be (a) two flows (a legacy/alt path vs. the current one),
(b) a pre-wizard contact gate feeding the wizard, or (c) a country/segment variant. Promoting a second
"submit application" UC would duplicate UC0001; assuming they are one flow could hide a real branch.
**Not resolvable from screenshots.**

**Evidence:** screencapture-patrondeti-cz-zadost-zadatel-2026-07-04-13_18_12.png vs.
screencapture-patrondeti-cz-zadost-formular-2026-07-04-13_18_33.png.

**What would resolve it:**
1. In `intake/current-solution/_source/patronus/`, identify the route/controller for both `/zadost/zadatel`
   and `/zadost-formular` — is one deprecated/redirecting? Do they share a session/aggregate (EN0003
   ApplicationSession)?
2. Determine what the **"Zpět na výběr"** selection step is (story-type / with-vs-without-Patron / country
   choice?).
3. Cross-check `intake/process-maps/` (ŽÁDOST FRONT area) for the current intake path.
4. **Outcome:** either fold `/zadost/zadatel` into UC0001 as an alternative/entry flow, or record a
   confirmed second flow — do **not** mint a duplicate UC before this is answered.

## OQ-02 — DMS SMS donation channel ("DMS TRV PATRONDETI 90/290 → 87 777") — **Uncertain**

**Gap ref:** G-17 (audit §c-9). **Verdict: Uncertain — single editorial mention; possible ES gap.**

A **donor SMS (DMS) donation channel** — including a **recurring** variant ("trvalá dárcovská SMS") —
is referenced **only** in one blog article. It appears in **no** UC, ES, or MSG. If real, it is a
payment path (and an external SMS/DMS aggregator boundary) not represented anywhere in the spec; the
ES synthesis explicitly noted "SMS (no dedicated gateway — likely none)".

**Why it matters.** A recurring-SMS donation path would be a genuine payment channel + external system
absent from UC0005/UC0007 and the ES map. But a single marketing mention is **not** sufficient evidence
to promote it.

**Evidence:** screencapture-patrondeti-cz-blog-patron-deti-nova-pomaha-a-ondrej-sokol-zvou-do-kampane-darujme-prazdniny-2026-07-04-13_17_24.png.

**What would resolve it:**
1. Search the Patronus source for any DMS/SMS/donor-SMS integration, aggregator, or short-code handling.
2. If present → promote a new **ES** (DMS/SMS aggregator) + extend **UC0005/UC0007**; if absent →
   record as an **externally-operated channel not integrated into Patronus** (donations arrive via a
   third-party DMS provider and land in bank reconciliation, UC0008) and close.
3. Reconcile with the ES-synthesis "SMS likely none" note.

výběr dítěte … na nás?" → "Nechám to na vás"), and CLAUDE.md notes multiple story types incl. group and

basic/promo/long-term/short-term) but **no explicit collection-account/group type**.

**Evidence:** screencapture-patrondeti-cz-2026-07-04-13_15_49.png;
screencapture-patrondeti-cz-2026-07-04-13_16_37.png.

**What would resolve it:** confirm the story-type enumeration in source/config (does a
collection-account/group type exist as a Campaign attribute?) and, if so, extend EN0004 + glossary C004.
Routed as follow-up **F-06**.
