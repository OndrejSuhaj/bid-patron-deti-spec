## Notes on uncertainty

- **Merged contexts (C10 + C11 → ARCH0012):** merged because both are thin / partially-mined (HS16),
  own no resident aggregate, and their core flows (FL055 search-sync, FL057 scheduled publish, FL059
  ops listener) are un-mined. Kept identifiable as sub-contexts inside the one doc.
- **Shared aggregate C7 ↔ C9:** AG7 Party is resident in C7 (ARCH0009) and shared by C9 (ARCH0011),
  which contributes auth/GDPR commands — reflected as a cross-reference, not a duplicated aggregate.
- **Dormant subsystem:** campaign recommendation (C3 / FN0024 / AG7 Account) is navigable but marked
  dormant everywhere it appears (ARCH0005, ARCH0009).
