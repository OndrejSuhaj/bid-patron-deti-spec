## 5. Boundary violations (aggregated — top architectural risks)
1. **Domain side-effects live in entity `preSave`/`postSave`** (Application, Transaction, Campaign) — any `save()` from any context triggers emails, Slack, campaign recompute, overpayment split, ES enqueue. Challenges every SRV boundary. `Evidence:` FLW0001, FLW0003–FLW0007.
2. **Payment callbacks are public** (`_permission: access content`); authenticity = shared secret in body (ComGate) or re-poll (MAIB); **no idempotence / replay guard**. `Evidence:` FLW0003, FLW0004, FLW0005.
3. **Path collision:** ComGate and MAIB both bind `/transaction/status_update` (`comgate.routing.yml` vs `maib.routing.yml`) — non-deterministic resolution. `Evidence:` FLW0003, FLW0005. `Hypothesis` on which wins.
4. **Raw-SQL cross-aggregate writes** bypass entity API/validation/revisions (scoring, notification, `application_states` INSERT, `SELECT MAX(int_id)` for contract id). `Evidence:` FLW0001, FLW0002, FLW0008.
5. **No transition-legality enforcement:** `ChangeModStateForm`/`WorkflowStatusForm` call `setState()` to any of ~66 states with no server-side transition/role check. `Evidence:` FLW0002.
6. **Optimistic PAID** in recurring cron (marks PAID before webhook confirms) + non-atomic 28-day dedup → double-charge risk. `Evidence:` FLW0007.
7. **`USE_QUEUE=FALSE`** — the "async" mailing queue runs synchronously in-request/in-save; external I/O can abort persistence. `Evidence:` FLW0001, FLW0008, FLW0009, FLW0010.
8. **Hardcoded secrets on live paths:** Slack webhook (TransactionEntity), MAIB cert passphrase, MobilPay password (NetopiaCron), WhoisXML apiKey (EmailService). `Evidence:` FLW0003, FLW0005, FLW0007, FLW0010 (values redacted).
