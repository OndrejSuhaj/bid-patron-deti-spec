## 1. Core domain concepts

Patronus is a **child-patronage / donation platform** running across CZ / RO / MD. The domain revolves around a
single heavyweight aggregate — the **Application (Žádost, EN0001)** — that binds together the parties (a
**fundraiser** who applies on behalf of a **child**, and a **patron** who funds the help), the risk gate that
must clear it, the **Contract (EN0011)** that legalises it, and the public **Campaign / Story (Příběh, EN0004)**
that raises the money. Money flows in as **Transactions (EN0009)** (one-off, corporate, recurring, voucher, or
bank/AISP imports), whose first transition to *paid* is the money-side hub that recomputes campaign funding,
promotes recurring schedules and vouchers, grants donor roles, and can auto-complete the whole case.

Two vocabulary overloads must be held explicitly. **"Lead" is not a separate entity**: it is the early
intake/coordination phase of the *same* Application record (carried by the lead-role / lead-origin / lead-contact
fields), so the ~66-label workflow spans both lead-era and application-era states on one field
[EN0001; FLW0025]. **"Patron" spans three records**: the patron *role* on a User (EN0008), the patron *Contact*
(EN0006), and the public patron *display profile* (EN0005) attached to a Campaign — distinct entities with the
same business word. Similarly **Account (EN0007)** is neither a bank account nor the Drupal user account, and its
recommendation machinery is **dormant** (INV27).

The concepts below are the kernel. They are grouped by the bounded contexts C1..C11 established in
`SRV-architecture-map.md` §1. Confidence is `Confirmed` unless noted.

## 3. Core state machines

Simplified, phase-grouped views of the lifecycles that carry the domain. Individual state **labels** are
Confirmed from evidence; **groupings/phases** are analytic. Hypothesis transitions are marked.

### 3.1 Application (Žádost) — core aggregate lifecycle [EN0001] — *Confidence: Partial*

**States (phase-grouped over the ~66-state workflow):** INTAKE `{new}` · RISK-REVIEW `{to_check, scoring,
scoring_ok}` · PROCESSING `{application_processing, waiting, suspended}` · CONTRACTING `{contract,
waiting_signature, contract_signed, waiting_for_feetback}` · RETURNED `{returned_new_patron}` · PUBLIC
`{active}` · TERMINAL `{complete/completed, duplicate}`.

**Critical current-state properties.** There is **one** entity and **one** `state` field — "Lead" is the early
phase of the same record, not a separate vocabulary; the ~66-label enum spans lead-era and application-era
labels on that one field [EN0001; FLW0025]. `state` and the Drupal moderation state are written in lock-step
[FLW0002]. **No transition-legality guard exists on 2 of the 3 admin forms** — they offer all ~66 states
unfiltered, so ANY state is reachable from ANY state; only one form filters offered options and even it
re-validates nothing server-side [FLW0002] (see HS02). Every save unconditionally raises the status event
fan-out even when the value is unchanged — **not idempotent** (duplicate audit rows) [FLW0002] (see HS01).
CZ/RO/MD share this single field/workflow; per-country differences live in reaction config, not in separate
state machines. Which states are terminal vs re-enterable is **undefined** because no legality guard exists
(Open Question, EN0001).

## 5. Traceability note

This kernel is a **single-source synthesis**. It references the EN (entities/invariants), UC
(behavior/orchestration) and FLW (mined-flow) layers **by id only** and does **not** restate their internal
detail nor modify them:

- **Concepts (§1)** are owned by their EN entities and behavioural UC use-cases; the kernel names each concept's
  owning EN, related UC, and bounded context (C1..C11 per `SRV-architecture-map.md` §1) and does not duplicate
  their field lists or field semantics.
- **Invariants (§2)** are cross-entity rules; each `INV` cites the owning EN entities plus the UC/FLW evidence
  that established it. Nothing here overrides an entity's own single-owned facts.
- **State machines (§3)** are **simplified** phase-grouped views over the workflows detailed in EN lifecycle
  sections and the FLW dossiers; the authoritative label sets and transition detail remain in those sources.
  Hypothesis / Partial transitions are marked and point back to the Open Questions that generated them.
- **Hotspots (§4)** re-index the rewrite-risk analysis by area and cite EN/UC/FLW/SRV evidence; they add no new
  claims beyond that evidence.

No file / class / method names appear in this document, and no raw table or column names are used as claims.
Domain-significant field and status semantics are expressed in **domain terms** and attributed to their owning
EN/FLW by id (single-source): where the underlying evidence is a raw-SQL write, a hardcoded value, or a specific
field/column, this kernel names the *behaviour* (e.g. "the campaign's running raised total", "the risk / blacklist
classification", "the paid/published state") and points to the EN/UC/FLW id that recorded the underlying
implementation detail, rather than restating the raw identifier here. The only literal tokens retained are the
domain **status / classification vocabulary** (Lead/Application/Story/Transaction status labels and the risk-tier
values) and named **external systems / integrations**, both of which are legitimate ubiquitous-language terms.
Confidence labels (`Confirmed` / `Partial` / `Hypothesis`) are carried through from the source artifacts
unchanged; conflicts and open questions are surfaced, not resolved.
