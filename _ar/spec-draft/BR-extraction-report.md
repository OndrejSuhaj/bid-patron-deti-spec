## 1. Active rules and template

- **Layer rules:** `tooling/docs/rules-BR.md` — BR is normative, deterministic,
  implementation-agnostic, free of workflow description; SHALL / MUST / SHALL NOT; no step-by-step
  flows, no implementation detail.
- **Cross-layer discipline:** `tooling/docs/cross-layer-discipline.md` — single-source ownership;
  reference other layers by `doc_id`; the >50-character restatement rule; skip-empty-sections;
  surface unknowns with the AR evidence vocabulary (`Confirmed` / `Partial` / `Uncertain` /
  `Blocked` / `Hypothesis`).
- **Template:** `tooling/templates/template-BR.md` — frontmatter (`doc_id`, `title`,
  `canonical_layer: BR`, `spec_type: business-rule`, `status`, `affects`, optional `references`) +
  `## Purpose` → rule sections → `## Non-Goals`.
- Precedence applied (per cross-layer §8): glossary (`_ar/repo-map/glossary.md`) → cross-layer
  discipline → rules-BR → template. No task-level formatting override was required.

  recurring/voucher/role/message/funding owners are referenced, not restated.
- **Transition-legality gap** — HS01, HS02, EN0001 Open Questions, and the §3.1 "any→any" rows all
  described the same gap. Merged into two current-state sections of BR-ApplicationStatusGovernance.
- **INV04 App↔Campaign consistency** — appeared on both the EN0001 and EN0004 sides plus §3.1/§3.2.
  Owned once by BR-ApplicationStatusGovernance; the alert-*delivery* facet split to
  BR-OperationalAlerting (mechanism vs. policy) to avoid restatement.
- **INV06 funded-rejection** — surfaced as both a Campaign rule and a Transaction rule. Placed once
  as a money-admissibility rule in BR-PaymentAndMoneyIntegrity; BR-CampaignStoryLifecycle references
  it.
- **INV17 merge hard-delete** — appeared as a party-merge mechanic and as a GDPR concern. Mechanics
  owned by BR-PartyIdentityAndDeduplication; the data-protection *consequence* stated once (by

- **Country-variance (HS11)** — country-specific rules (CZ confirmation, CZ reconciliation, RO

  no-country-filter scoping owned by BR-MultiTenantCountryScoping; each references the other once.
- **INV20 cardinality** — the ≤2-profile (case) facet stays with the Application aggregate governance;
  the ≤1-public-patron (campaign) facet kept in BR-CampaignStoryLifecycle. Split by owning aggregate,
  not duplicated.

## 7. Ambiguities / current-state-not-enforced rules of note

The catalog deliberately captures **absence of enforcement** as faithful current-state rules (not
target proposals). Notable ones a reader must not mistake for design intent:

- **No transition-legality guard** (BR-ApplicationStatusGovernance) — any workflow status reachable
  from any status on most change surfaces; terminal vs. re-enterable undefined (HS02).
- **Non-idempotent status fan-out** (BR-ApplicationStatusGovernance, BR-TransactionalMessaging) —
  unchanged re-save can re-log and re-notify (HS01/HS13).
- **No payment identity uniqueness / no idempotency key** (BR-PaymentAndMoneyIntegrity, INV25) and
  **non-signed, replayable gateway callbacks** (BR-PaymentGatewayCallbacks, HS12).
- **Optimistic PAID on recurring charge** booking unconfirmed money (BR-RecurringDonationPolicy,
  HS04).
- **Non-unique voucher codes + TOCTOU double-apply** (BR-VoucherPolicy, INV24).
- **Destructive, non-transactional merges** hard-deleting users/history (BR-PartyIdentityAndDedup,
  INV17/HS09) and **incomplete / anti-erasure GDPR** (BR-DataProtectionAndErasure, HS06).
- **Unfiltered cross-tenant PII exports** (BR-MultiTenantCountryScoping + BR-ReportingAndDataAccess,
  HS07) and **no tenant/country column** on core entities (HS11).
- **Cross-profile owner-coupling in scoring** — low-risk score reads fundraiser-profile fields
  (BR-ScoringAndRiskGating, INV16); recorded as a current-state anomaly, not corrected.
- **App-level-only contract numbering uniqueness** — race-prone (BR-ContractAndESignature, INV22).
- **Dormant recommendation subsystem** — inert on 5 grounds; stated as current-state, explicitly not
  target behaviour (BR-CampaignRecommendationDormant, INV27/HS14).
- **Partial-evidence items** carried through with their labels: RO tax-redirect pairing (INV26,
  Partial), organisation/search index sync (FN0022/HS16, Partial), reporting dashboard read-model
  (Partial).
